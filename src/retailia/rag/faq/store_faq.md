# Retailia store FAQ

This FAQ is synthetic demo content for the Retailia example store.

## Returns and refunds

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Start a return from the Orders page and choose "Return item"; we email a prepaid label to the address on your account. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the parcel reaches our warehouse. Final-sale items, opened beauty products and gift cards cannot be returned.

## Exchanges

To exchange an item for a different size or colour, start a return and place a new order. This is the fastest way to get the replacement, because the new item ships right away instead of waiting for the return to arrive.

## Shipping options and delivery times

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders over $50. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $12.99. Orders placed before 2:00 PM (14:00) Eastern time on a business day ship the same day. We currently ship within the United States only.

## Tracking an order

When your order ships we email a tracking code that starts with TRK. You can also ask this assistant "where is my order?" or look at the Orders page. Tracking details usually appear within 24 hours of the shipping email; see https://example.com/track for the carrier's tracking page.

## Cancelling an order

Orders can be cancelled while their status is "processing". Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but you can return it after delivery.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and store gift cards. Your card is charged when the order ships, not when it is placed. We never ask for your full card number or password by chat or email.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact support within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We send a replacement or a full refund at no cost to you, including return shipping.

## Sizing help

Each clothing product page has a size chart in centimetres and inches. If you are between sizes we recommend the larger size. Exchanges for a different size are free within 30 days.

## Warranty

Electronics come with a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects. Keep your order number; it serves as proof of purchase.

## Contacting support

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM Eastern time, by email at support@example.com or by phone at 555-0100. Average email response time is under 24 hours.

## Account and password

You can reset your password from the sign-in page with "Forgot password". For your security, support staff will never ask for your password. You can update your shipping address under Account settings.

## Privacy

We only use your personal data to process orders and provide support. This assistant can see your own orders, cart and account summary, and never shares information about other customers.
